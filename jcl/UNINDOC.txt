1. OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document provides step-by-step instructions to completely remove
the Multi-User To-Do Manager (TODO) from your MVS 3.8j (TK5) system.
The companion teardown JCL resides in the same PDS: TODO.JCL(UNINSTAL).

2. PRE-UNINSTALL PREPARATION (QUIESCE ACTIVE USERS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before submitting the uninstall job, verify that no TSO users are
currently running KICKS (%TODOKICK) or the TSO CLI (%TODO):
  - Step CHKUSE in TODO.JCL(UNINSTAL) allocates TODO.MASTER.CLUSTER
    with DISP=OLD. If any online session holds the file open, the job
    will wait or fail safely before deleting any datasets.

3. RUNNING THE UNINSTALL JOB
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Submit the teardown job from TSO READY or RFE Option 3.4:

  SUBMIT 'TODO.JCL(UNINSTAL)'

The job runs with MSGCLASS=X (held output) and executes five steps:
  - Step 1 (CHKUSE)  : Verifies exclusive access to TODO.MASTER.CLUSTER.
  - Step 2 (DELVSAM) : Deletes PATH1, AIX1, MASTER.CLUSTER,
                       CONTROL.CLUSTER, all generations and base of
                       TODO.ARCHIVE.GDG, and SYS2.CMDPROC hooks
                       (TODO and TODOKICK).
  - Step 3 (DELPDS)  : Deletes all ten TODO.* PDS libraries on PUB000.
  - Step 4 (DELALIAS): Removes the 'TODO' alias from the Master Catalog.
  - Step 5 (CHKLIST) : Writes a post-uninstall verification checklist
                       to the held SYSOUT listing.

4. POST-UNINSTALL VERIFICATION & MANUAL CHECKLIST
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] Inspect job UNINSTAL in RFE Option 3.8 (Outlist) and confirm
      RC=0000 across all steps.
  [ ] In RFE Option 3.4, search DSNAME level 'TODO' to verify zero
      orphan datasets remain cataloged or on volume PUB000.
  [ ] In RFE Option 3.4, verify members TODO and TODOKICK are no
      longer present in SYS2.CMDPROC.
  [ ] If your site added custom RAKF security rules for 'TODO.**',
      remove those entries and refresh RAKF.
  [ ] If TODO.JCL(TODONITE) was added to an automated batch schedule,
      remove that schedule entry.
